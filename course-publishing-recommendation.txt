Course Publishing Recommendation

Do not automatically change a published course to Draft when an instructor edits it.

Recommended enterprise approach:

1. Keep the current published version unchanged for learners.
2. Create a new draft version when editing starts.
3. Allow instructors to edit, preview, and validate the draft privately.
4. Publish the draft only after validation succeeds.
5. Replace the learner-visible version atomically during publishing.

Example:

Published version: v3 - visible to learners
Draft version: v4 - being edited and validated

After publishing:

Published version: v4 - visible to learners

The course should remain Published, but show an indicator such as
"Unpublished changes" until the new version is successfully published.

This prevents learners from seeing incomplete or unvalidated changes while
allowing instructors to work safely without taking the course offline.
